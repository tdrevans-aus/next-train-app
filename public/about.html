About Next Train

Next Train is the leave-by companion for Perth rail. It tells you when to walk out the door for your train — using live Transperth times, delays included — not just another departure board.

Near me shows what’s leaving from the station nearest you. My Journeys saves the commute you repeat (station, direction, and how long you need to get to the platform) and turns that into a leave-by time. Preferences stay on this device.

Android extras

In the Android app you can add a home-screen widget and optional leave reminders (including a live countdown in your notifications) so leave-by stays visible without opening the app. All are opt-in from Menu.

Not official

Next Train is an independent app from Evans Studios. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Transperth or the Public Transport Authority of Western Australia (PTA). It uses publicly available live data. Always check platform displays and announcements at the station.

Ads & remove ads

The free app may show a small banner ad (Google AdMob on Android; Google AdSense on the web when configured) to help cover hosting.

On Android, you can remove ads with a one-time Unlock Pro purchase from Menu when that option is available. We never see your card details — Google Play handles payment.
