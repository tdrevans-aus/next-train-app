About Next Train

Next Train tells you when to leave home for your next train — using live Transperth data, with delays included.

Set up your journeys once; the app remembers your stations, directions, and timing on this device.

Not official

This app is not affiliated with Transperth or the PTA. It uses publicly available live data. Always check platform displays and announcements at the station.

Ads

The free app may show a small banner ad (Google AdMob on Android, AdSense on the web) to help cover hosting costs. Remove ads forever with a one-time purchase from Menu in the Android app.
