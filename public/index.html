        Help

        My Journeys

        A saved regular trip — your station, direction, and time to station. Set them up via the icon.

        More than one journey (e.g. morning in and evening out)? Tap Morning into town under the station name to switch. To add or edit, tap the edit icon beside the journey name (or tap My Journeys again at the top).

        Next Train card

        The top card shows when your train departs. Swipe left for later departures, right for earlier ones.

        Near me

        Tap in the top bar for the nearest-station board. Pick a direction from the list, or swipe for other departures. Tap Near me again to refresh your location.

        Pinning a train

        On Near me, tap the pin icon (top right of the departure card) to hold that train on screen until about a minute after it leaves — or until you unpin.

        While pinned, a Leave by card appears, Other directions hides to free space, and the train shows on the home screen widget. Turn on Tell me when to leave if you want a leave reminder — pin alone doesn’t ask for notification permission.

        Leave by

        On a journey with target train on, the Leave by card shows when to head out based on your time to station.

        If you’re late, tap I’ve left or Next Train (same as swiping left). On Near me and pinned Routes, Leave by appears when a train is pinned; tap Hide to clear it anytime.

        If location is allowed and the card says leave now or you’re overdue, the app may auto-dismiss it when you’re moving or near the station — no extra popup.

        Reminders

        Turn on Target train and set the time. Remind me fires a Leave now alarm at leave-by time (sound + notification shade until dismissed).

        Tap I’ve left for a quiet countdown while you walk to the station. Pause all reminders anytime from Menu → Pause reminders. The app picks the first train at or after your target time.

        Platform & status

        Live platform and delay info for the train you’re viewing.

        Direction

        Pick what the platform sign says — the end of the line — not necessarily where you get off.

        Active hours

        Optional times when a journey is selected automatically — for example, morning 6:00–9:00 switches to your commute-in journey without you having to tap.

        Home screen widget

        Add from Menu so your next train shows on the home screen without opening the app. Long-press to resize for a roomier layout.
