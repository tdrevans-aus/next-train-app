      How it works

      • Journeys — A saved regular trip: your station, direction, and leave-home buffer. Set them up in . More than one — e.g. morning in and evening out? Tap Daily Commute - in under the station name to change which journey you’re viewing.
      • Next Train — The top card shows when your train departs. Swipe left or right for later or earlier departures.
      • Leave by — When leave-home buffer is on, the card below shows when to head out. Tap the sliders icon on that card to change the buffer. Untick buffer in journey editor to hide it.
      • Near me — Tap in the top bar for the nearest-station board. Pick a direction from the list, or swipe for other departures. Tap again to refresh your location.
      • Platform & status — Live platform and delay info for the train you’re viewing.
      • Direction — Pick what the platform sign says (end of the line), not necessarily where you get off.
      • Active hours — Optional times when a journey is selected automatically (e.g. morning 6:00–9:00).
