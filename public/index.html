    How it works

    • Train departs — Live countdown to your train. Swipe the card left or right to see later or earlier departures.
    • Leave by — When leave-home buffer is on, shows when to head out. Tap the sliders icon on that card to change it.
    • Direction — Pick what the platform sign says (the end of the line), not necessarily where you get off.
    • Leave-home buffer — Minutes you need to reach the platform before the train. Untick to hide the leave card.
    • Default hours — Optional times when a journey is selected automatically (e.g. morning 6:00–9:00). Leave blank for manual only.
    • Then — The next train after the one you are viewing.
