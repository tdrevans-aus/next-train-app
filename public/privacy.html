Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Next Train (“the app”) helps you see live train departure times for Perth public transport. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

Your station, direction, and timing preferences are saved in your browser’s localStorage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Train times

Live train data is fetched from Transperth’s public live-times service via our API. We do not receive your personal identity as part of that request.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

If you accept cookies/ads, we use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

The free version shows ads. Remove ads with a one-time purchase from Menu in the Android app (handled by Google Play — we do not receive your card details).

Learn how Google uses data: Google partner sites policy

Hosting

The app is hosted on a cloud provider (e.g. Vercel or Netlify). Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, request time) may be collected by the host for security and operations.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site operator via the details on the About page.

Disclaimer

This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Transperth or the Public Transport Authority of Western Australia. Train times are unofficial and provided without guarantee.
