Tester feedback — design

About → one link → one short form. No screenshots, no categories, no accounts. Built for friends / closed testers, not App Store review spam.

Delivery recommendation

Use a hosted form endpoint that emails you (Formspree / Getform / Basin, or a tiny Vercel route → email). Not a database, not Sentry User Feedback, not raw mailto:.

  • mailto: opens Gmail/Outlook, loses context, high abandon — fine as a fallback link only.
  • Google Form: fastest to ship for testing, but leaves the app and looks off-brand.
  • Own DB / Notion / Sheet: overkill until volume hurts; email inbox is enough for <50 testers.
  • Auto-attach: app version + platform (Android/web). No location, no journey names unless they type them.
