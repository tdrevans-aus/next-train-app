## v1 mode cut

**MTR urban heavy-rail only — lines the current Next Train spec (v1.7) actually covers, minus Airport Express and Disneyland Resort:** **ISL** Island Line (Kennedy Town–Chai Wan), **TWL** Tsuen Wan Line (Tsuen Wan–Central), **KTL** Kwun Tong Line (Whampoa–Tiu Keng Leng), **TKL** Tseung Kwan O Line (North Point–Po Lam / LOHAS Park), **TCL** Tung Chung Line (Hong Kong–Tung Chung), **TML** Tuen Ma Line (Wu Kai Sha–Tuen Mun), **EAL** East Rail Line (Admiralty–Lo Wu / Lok Ma Chau), **SIL** South Island Line (Admiralty–South Horizons). Official EN print on the system-map page / homepage line lists: Island Line, Tsuen Wan Line, Kwun Tong Line, Tseung Kwan O Line, Tung Chung Line, Tuen Ma Line, East Rail Line, South Island Line. **Tuen Ma, East Rail, and South Island are in v1.7 and live on the REST** (EAL-ADM / SIL-ADM / TML-HUH **200** 2026-08-29) — in. East Rail **Lo Wu / Lok Ma Chau** are EAL border stations on that line, not High Speed Rail and not a mainland through-train product. **Out:** **AEL** Airport Express (in v1.7 and live — same endpoint; AEL-HOK **200** 2026-08-29 — that is the trap); **DIS** Disneyland Resort Line (in v1.7 — out); Light Rail / NWNT (separate `lrt/getSchedule` + separate official map); High Speed Rail / 高鐵 (Hong Kong West Kowloon — on the mixed system-map plate; **not** in Next Train v1.7); Ngong Ping 360; MTR Buses / feeder buses; KMB / Citybus; ferries; trams; Peak Tram; through-train to mainland. Do not invent a Light Rail city id.

Hub lock: **Admiralty** (official print; spec **ADM**). Official line lists + live REST: **TWL × ISL × SIL × EAL**. Not Central (TWL × ISL only; spec **CEN**), not Tsim Sha Tsui (TWL; **TST**), not East Tsim Sha Tsui (TML; **ETS**), not Hung Hom (EAL × TML; **HUH**), not Kowloon (Airport Express + Tung Chung; spec **KOW**), not Hong Kong station (Airport Express + Tung Chung; spec **HOK**), not Hong Kong West Kowloon (HSR), not Downtown. Tuen Ma, Kwun Tong, Tseung Kwan O, and Tung Chung do not serve Admiralty. There is no station on all eight v1 lines — Admiralty is the inner TWL/ISL/SIL/EAL lock. doNotGroup Admiralty vs Central (Island / Tsuen Wan) vs Tsim Sha Tsui vs East Tsim Sha Tsui vs Hung Hom vs Kowloon (Airport Express) vs Hong Kong station (Airport Express / Tung Chung) vs Hong Kong West Kowloon HSR vs Exhibition Centre vs Tsim Sha Tsui / East Tsim Sha Tsui interchange walk.
