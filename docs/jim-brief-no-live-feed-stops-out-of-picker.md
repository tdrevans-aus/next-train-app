## Decision and why
Tram / metro / subway stops whose operator has no confirmed live-departures feed (Manchester Metrolink, Nottingham NET, Tyne and Wear Metro, Sheffield Supertram, Glasgow Subway, Edinburgh Trams) currently sit in the station picker and can be chosen by Near me, and every such choice ends in the PR #329 message ("Live times for Manchester Metrolink aren't available yet…"). That was the only way to explain the gap before PR #327 gave Help a per-region "What's covered" entry and a "Can't find your station?" row in the picker. A selectable stop that can never produce a board is a dead end; the walk-up rule (`docs/board-eligibility-rule.md`) is about not hiding services at stations we show, not about listing stations we can't serve. So: keep the stops in the data (they must reappear the day a feed lands, and coverage notes and Near me need to know they exist), but stop offering them.

## Change
1. **Data flag, one place.** In each affected region's `lib/cities/<city>/stations.json`, add `"liveFeed": false` to every stop whose board throws a `FeedUnconfirmedError` subclass today (all Metrolink, NET, T&W Metro, Supertram, Subway, Edinburgh Trams entries). Add a short dated `notes` line per pack. Derive, don't hand-list: write a small `scripts/list-no-live-feed-stops.mjs` that walks the six regions and reports every `mode: metro` stop lacking a live provider, and use its output to set the flag, so the two can be compared by a gate.
2. **Serve the flag.** `api/city-stations.js` and the bundled `public/city-catalogs/*.json` / `parseCatalogRows` in `public/brisbane-dogfood.js` carry `liveFeed` through (default `true` when absent, so every other city is untouched).
3. **Picker.** `public/station-combobox.js`: stations with `liveFeed === false` are not offered in any picker (Near me fallback, journey detail, routes). The "Can't find your station?" row and the "?" icon from #327 stay as the explanation path. Journeys already saved on such a stop (unlikely, but possible from before) keep working through the #329 message — don't delete rider data.
4. **Near me.** `findNearestStation` in `public/app.js` and the server-side `findNearestStation` in `lib/cities/live-city-api.js` skip `liveFeed === false` stops when choosing the nearest station, so a rider at Altrincham resolves to the nearest served station or to the "You're in Manchester — choose a station below" picker (#318), never to a dead board. Keep the coordinates: the coverage note and future re-enable need them.
5. **Coverage notes.** Update the six regions' `lib/cities/<city>/coverage.json` "partial" entry to say the stops are not offered yet and why (rider prose, per `docs/jim-brief-help-coverage-notes.md`).
6. **Gates.** The six dogfood gates currently assert that dispatch "correctly surfaces <X>FeedUnconfirmedError rather than the static label list" — keep that assertion (the provider behaviour is unchanged and #329's copy still guards it) and add: every `mode: metro` stop in the region carries `liveFeed: false`, and the picker/nearest helpers exclude them. Add `qa/no-live-feed-stops-gate.mjs` (smoke tier): for each of the six regions, the script from step 1 and the flags in stations.json agree exactly; `api/city-stations` returns the flag; a Playwright check in one region (greater-manchester) that Altrincham does not appear in the Near me picker and that Help's coverage entry mentions Metrolink. Update `qa/feed-unconfirmed-rider-copy.mjs` so it still hits the six boards directly via `/api/board` (that path stays as the safety net).
7. **Docs.** One dated paragraph in `docs/board-eligibility-rule.md` recording the refinement: a station with no servable board is filtered *out of the picker*, with its verdict recorded in the region's coverage note and pack notes; this replaces the 2 Sep 2026 error-surfacing approach as the rider-facing treatment, while the explicit error remains the API behaviour. Propagation-log line in `docs/united-kingdom-ledger.md`.
