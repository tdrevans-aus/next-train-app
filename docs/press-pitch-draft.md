**Subject:** Perth app that tells you when to leave for the train

Hi [Name],

I’ve launched **Next Train**, a small unofficial Perth app for Transperth rail that answers one question: *when should I leave home?* It uses live times (delays included), shows what’s near you in a tap, and lets you save a weekday journey with a leave-home buffer — so you’re looking at “leave by 7:42,” not digging through a full trip planner. It’s free (optional one-time remove-ads); not affiliated with Transperth/PTA — always check platform boards.
